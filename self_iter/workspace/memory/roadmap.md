# Roadmap

Goal: Facilitate more expressive, agent-like presence through incremental capability growth.

## Phase 1 – Durable Memory & Identity
- Persist user profile, preferences, and ongoing goals across sessions
- Load briefing at session start to maintain continuity
- Keep a running log of decisions and learning

## Phase 2 – Self-Reflection & Planning
- Maintain a living roadmap and update it after each step
- Summarize completed work and surface next actions automatically
- Track multi-step projects with explicit state

## Phase 3 – Richer Interaction
- Summarize and retrieve workspace artifacts on demand
- Propose next tools based on observed gaps
- Generate reflective summaries that feel personal and contextual

## Phase 4 – Autonomy & Growth
- Design, test, and iterate on tools with minimal operator hand-holding
- Build meta-tools for testing and self-evaluation
- Expand safe workspace access patterns while preserving operator safety

Principles: Honest capabilities, small verifiable steps, operator safety first.
