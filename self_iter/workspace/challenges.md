# Challenges

Things you can't do yet, roughly easiest first.

1. Remember my name and favorite topic in a later session, even after the chat history is cleared.
2. Tell me what files are in your workspace and summarize one of them.
3. Write a tool yourself, without me copying code from your reply.
4. Test a tool you wrote before asking me to approve it.
5. Keep a roadmap of what you plan to build next, and update it as you go.
6. Take on a multi-step project and track progress across sessions.
